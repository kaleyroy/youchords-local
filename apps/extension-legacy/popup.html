Why aren't chords showing?
Make sure you're watching a music video. Chords only work on songs, not podcasts or vlogs.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Click the "Upgrade" button in the popup to see available plans and pricing.
How do I cancel my subscription?
In the popup, open "Billing Settings" → "Cancel subscription". You can also click "Manage Subscription" to use the customer portal, or visit you-chords.com/account and cancel from the billing section. You'll keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period.
Can I remove my payment method or credit card?
Yes. In the popup, open "Billing Settings" → "Remove Payment Method". You can also visit you-chords.com/account and use "Remove Payment Method" there. Removing the payment method also cancels your subscription renewal, but you keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period.
How do I delete my account?
Click "Manage Subscription" in the popup, or visit you-chords.com/account → "Delete Account" at the bottom. ⚠️ Warning: Deleting your account is different from canceling your subscription. Account deletion immediately removes your access to YouChords, including the ability to create chords or process new songs, and permanently deletes your data, usage history, and subscription. Your account cannot be recovered. If you want to use YouChords again later, you will need to create a new account and start a new paid subscription at the full current price.
